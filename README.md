## Primary deliverables:

- # Wire rod cooling study (product portfolio development and study project): A comprehensive 1-D Finite Difference Method (FDM) model and a 3-D Computational Fluid Dynamics model (CFD) towards predicting the (volumetric) temperature history of wire coils in a coil cooling conveyor and predicting the temperature field of air at reduced-order conditions with phase-change approximations, respectively, using Conjugate Heat Transfer (CHT), under ambient conditions, supported with appropriate experimental work, towards achieving a variable range of mechanical properties for wire coils of different coils as per end-consumer requirements. 1-D model extended to accommodate coil-level radiation exchange with radiation shields to slow cooling rates. Attained 1.78% prediction error between 1-D, 3-D models and (preliminary) design-support experiments, supporting design of temperature control system for flexible variation in mechanical properties.

- CFD subdomains for the wire rod cooling study: Conjugate Heat Transfer (CHT), basic geometry and mesh cleanup (Geometry: Boolean combine/subtract, enclosure, share topology; ANSYS Mechanical Meshing: Proximity meshing and basic inflation layer creation (for laminar flow)), basic solver study and choice (coupled vs SIMPLE, PRESTO! over second-order for buoyant flows), S2S radiation
 
- # Technoeconomic feasibility study of retrofitting coal-fired reheating furnaces: Enabling an exploratory technoeconomic feasibility study of retrofitting coal-fired reheating furnaces to gas-fired reheating furnaces using 0D modelling with CANTERA.

- ROM subdomains for the technoeconomic feasibility study: CANTERA, CRECK mechanism developed by Stagni et al for ammonia combustion.
 
- # Laminar, transitional and turbulent rod cooling (V&V) study: ROM 1-D FDM modelling and CFD modelling of a cooling steel rod with phase change approximated using Eurocode-3 (EN!993 1-2) correlations and in-house proprietary correlations for thermophysical properties of steel. Verification and validation for natural convection case with on-field experiments with quantified GCI metrics for mesh sensitivity. Verification of forced convection axial and crossflow cases with quantified GCI metrics for mesh sensitivity.

- CFD subdomains for the rod cooling (V&V) study: Comparison of various turbulence models such as k-epsilon, k-omega families and transitional for transitional axial flow case; k-epsilon, k-omega families and LES for turbulent crossflow cooling case; ANSYS Fluent meshing with watertight geometry; Richardson Extrapolation Technique and GCI (Grid Convergence Index) for mesh sensitivity study; Assisted SCM automation through Fluent TUI console.  

- # Demonstration of various applications of combustion in the steel sector with ROM and CFD: 1-D and 2-D combustion simulations using CANTERA and ANSYS Fluent respectively for various applications in steel sector such as vertical shaft furnaces for DRI using various blends and an extension of deliverable 3.
